# Privacy

Inkling has no account and no analytics. Your subjects, timetable, study, tasks, trials, marks, spells, questions and creatures stay on your phone, in the app's own storage. Android's backup may keep a copy, as with any app, if you turned it on; a backup file you save yourself goes wherever you put it.

Only one thing goes over the network: **the weather for the tower window.** The coordinates of your town (guessed once from your time zone) are sent to Open-Meteo, or to MET Norway when Open-Meteo can't be reached. Nothing else goes with them.

**Photos of the board** are taken with your phone's own camera app and kept in Inkling's private storage. **Dictation** in «Teach the familiar» uses your phone's speech recognition; what you say goes wherever that service sends it, and Inkling keeps only the text. **The candle** uses the motion sensor only to notice that the phone lies face down; nothing is recorded.

Share cards leave the phone only when you share them yourself.
